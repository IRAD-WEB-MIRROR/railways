Vacancies

The railway takes on staff for the depot, the track distances, the stations and the trains. Posts are filled by competition; railway training is given at the enterprise's own technical school at the Nurzhan depot. Working clothes are issued free, and staff and their families travel on the railway's trains at a reduced fare.

Open competitions

TJ-26/16

Station duty officer

Place of work
Aulie-Ata Junction
Grade
4,100 som a month; shift work; service housing at the station
Applications until
11 December 2026

Receiving and sending trains, the run-round of train No. 1/2, the points and signals of the station, the station log and contact with the central dispatching office.

Requirements: secondary specialised railway education or experience as a station operator; Russian and Iradi; aged 21 to 50.

How to apply

Applications are accepted until 17:00 on the closing date. The application is addressed to the Director-General and handed in at the personnel department (Nurzhan station, left wing, room 112) or at the office of the station master at Talas, or sent by e-mail to hr@railways.iradgov.org with the reference of the vacancy in the subject line.

Documents: the application; a curriculum vitae; a copy of the passport or residence card; copies of diplomas; the work record book, if any; two photographs 3×4 cm. Candidates are invited to an interview and sent to the railway medical board.

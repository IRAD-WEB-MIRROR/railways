Written appeals

Written appeals are addressed to the Director-General and handed in at the chancellery (left wing, room 201), sent by post to Nurzhan station or by e-mail to info@railways.iradgov.org. An appeal is answered within 30 days; a complaint about service on a train within 15 days. Please give your name, address and, for a journey, the date, train and carriage number.

Every station with a ticket window and every carriage of train No. 1/2 keeps a book of complaints and suggestions, which is given to a passenger on request.

How to get here

Nurzhan station stands on Vokzal Square a few hundred metres south-west of the central square; marshrutkas stop on the square. The Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot is about 4 km north-east of the centre, on the north line.

Talas station is about 1.4 km south of the central square of Talas.

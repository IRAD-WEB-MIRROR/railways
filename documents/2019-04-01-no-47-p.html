Order · 1 April 2019 · No. 47-P

Rules for the carriage of passengers, luggage and hand luggage

In force

1. Tickets for train No. 1/2 are sold for cash at the station windows from ten days before the day of the journey until the departure of the train. Each ticket gives a numbered seat in the carriage shown on it.

2. Suburban tickets are sold at the window or, at stations without a window, by the conductor on the train without a surcharge.

3. Children under 7 travel free without a seat of their own; children from 7 to 15 years pay half the fare. Pensioners pay half the fare on suburban trains on showing the pension book.

4. On train No. 1/2 each passenger may carry 36 kg of hand luggage free. Heavier bags, sacks and bicycles are paid for at the rate fixed by the enterprise and placed where the conductor directs.

5. Bedding is issued by the conductor for a charge. The conductor sells tea.

6. Livestock, fuel, gas cylinders, weapons and explosive or poisonous substances may not be carried on passenger trains. Small animals and birds may be carried in a closed cage or basket in the passenger's own place.

7. A passenger who misses the train may return the ticket at the window within three hours of the departure and receive the fare less a charge of one quarter.

Director-General P. Yugai

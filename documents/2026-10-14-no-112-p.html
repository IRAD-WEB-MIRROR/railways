Order · 14 October 2026 · No. 112-P

On the passenger timetable from 1 November 2026

In force

In order to let passengers of the evening suburban trains reach the villages of Chu District before dark in the winter months, I ORDER:

1. To approve the passenger timetable from 1 November 2026. The times of trains No. 1 and No. 2 Talas – Nurzhan and of the suburban and depot workers' trains are unchanged, except as set out in point 2.

2. From 1 November to 31 March each year, train 6005 Nurzhan – Akjol to depart Nurzhan at 17:10 (arrive Akjol 18:20) and train 6006 Akjol – Nurzhan to depart Akjol at 18:35 (arrive Nurzhan 19:45). From 1 April they return to the summer times (17:50 and 19:15).

3. The passenger services department to post the timetable at all stations and on the railway's site by 27 October 2026 and to issue a printed timetable.

4. Control over the execution of this order is entrusted to the deputy director-general for traffic.

Director-General P. Yugai

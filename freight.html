Freight

Freight is most of the railway's work: refined products from the Nurzhan refinery, grain, refrigerated beef from the export zone at Talas, antimony and cotton lint. Nearly all of it goes to or comes from the Kazakh network, with which trains are exchanged at the Talas North Terminal and the Nurzhan rail border station.

Working today

Check a consignment

Enter the number of the railway consignment note to see where the wagons are. Consignments handed over to Kazakhstan Temir Zholy are shown up to the border.

Freight notices

Sending freight

Wagons are ordered from the commercial office of the Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot by the 20th of the month before. Most international consignments travel in Kazakh or private wagons, and forwarders book space on the trains. Through tariffs with Kazakhstan Temir Zholy are agreed each year; the tariff book for 2026 can be seen at the commercial office.

Freight stations

  • Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot, 4 km north-east of the city on the north line: the main yard, open round the clock.
  • Talas: goods office 08:00–17:00; the export zone's refrigerated container sidings west of Semiz.
  • Kara-Bulak: grain, beside the silos of Nurzhan Agro-Industrial.
  • Talas North Terminal: rail and road customs on the frontier facing Shymkent.
  • Nurzhan rail border station: exchange with Kazakhstan Temir Zholy towards Otar and Almaty.

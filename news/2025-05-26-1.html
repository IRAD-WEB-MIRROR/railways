26 May 2025 · No. 12

Safety at level crossings: joint checks with the Gendarmerie

From 26 May to 6 June staff of the track distances and patrols of the Gendarmerie will check the observance of the rules at level crossings on the Chu line and the Talas branch. Drivers must stop before the crossing when the signal is red or a train is approaching, and lorries with long loads must not stop on the crossing.

Before the school holidays the railway again reminds parents that the track is no place for children.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

Before the summer holidays: the railway is not a playground

Before the school holidays, staff of the track distances and the stations are visiting schools in Nurzhan, Akjol, Karasu and Kara-Bulak to talk to pupils about safety on the railway. Walking along the track, crossing in front of a train, climbing on wagons and playing near the points are dangerous: a diesel train needs several hundred metres to stop.

Parents are asked to remind their children of these rules. Herders driving stock over the line should use the level crossings.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

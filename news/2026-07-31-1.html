Railway Workers' Day: open day at the Nurzhan depot

Railway Workers' Day falls this year on Sunday 2 August. On Saturday 1 August, from 10:00 to 14:00, the Nurzhan depot will be open to staff, their families and school pupils: visitors may climb into the cab of a 2TE10 locomotive and one of the new shunters.

Veterans of the railway are invited to the station's hall of culture on 2 August at 12:00.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

Annual Report 2023: 5.5 million tonnes of freight

The enterprise has published its Annual Report for 2023. In the year the railway carried about 5.5 million tonnes of freight and about 180,000 passengers. Most of the freight went to or came from the Kazakh network: refined products from the Nurzhan refinery, grain, refrigerated beef from the Talas export zone, antimony and cotton lint.

The report is available in the documents section and at the chancellery.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

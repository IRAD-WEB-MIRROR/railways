Ten years of the State Enterprise Irad Temir Jolu

In January 2008 the State Enterprise Irad Temir Jolu was formed from the Territorial Railway Administration. In ten years it has kept the daily train between Talas and Nurzhan running, opened the refrigerated container sidings of the Talas export zone and renewed the exchange of trains with Kazakhstan at the two border stations.

The enterprise thanks its staff, its veterans and the shippers and passengers who use the railway.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

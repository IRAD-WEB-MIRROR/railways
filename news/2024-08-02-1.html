2 August 2024 · No. 18

Railway Workers' Day 2024

On the eve of Railway Workers' Day, Sunday 4 August, the Director-General signed an order thanking the staff of the enterprise. Thirty-five workers received certificates of honour and bonuses; the best locomotive crew of the year is from the Nurzhan depot, the best station Kara-Bulak.

Temirjolchu Nurzhan, the railway workers' club, will hold an open training session for children at the Lokomotiv Stadium on 4 August at 11:00.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

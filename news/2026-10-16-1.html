Winter timetable: the evening suburban trains to Akjol earlier from 1 November

By order No. 112-P of 14 October 2026, from 1 November until 31 March train 6005 will leave Nurzhan for Akjol at 17:10 instead of 17:50 and train 6006 will leave Akjol at 18:35 instead of 19:15, so that passengers reach the villages before dark. The other trains keep their times.

A printable timetable from 1 November is available on the timetable page.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

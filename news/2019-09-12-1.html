New shunting locomotives arrive at the Nurzhan depot

The first two of four new Chinese-built shunting locomotives bought by the enterprise this year arrived at the Nurzhan depot through the Nurzhan rail border station. They are the first new locomotives on the railway since the Soviet period.

After running-in and the training of crews they will work at the Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot and on the private sidings. The other two are expected before the end of the year.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

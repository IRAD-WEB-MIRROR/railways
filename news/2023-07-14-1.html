Twelve new assistant drivers complete their course

Twelve students of the technical school of the Nurzhan depot passed their examinations as assistant drivers of diesel locomotives. The course lasted ten months and included practice on the 2TE10 and TEM2 locomotives under experienced drivers.

All twelve have joined the locomotive crews of the depot. Enrolment for the next course is open; see the vacancies page.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

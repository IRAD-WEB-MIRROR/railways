4 December 2019 · No. 29

All four new shunters in service

The last two of the four new shunting locomotives were put into service at the Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot on 3 December. The Minister of Transport, Oleg Rudenko, attended the handing over at the depot.

The new locomotives take over the heaviest shunting at the freight depot; the TEM2 shunters they release will be overhauled in turn.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

Talas station: waiting hall reopened after roof repairs

The waiting hall of Talas station reopened on 2 October after the repair of its roof, which began in June. During the work, tickets were sold from the left-luggage room. The hall has been repainted and its benches renewed.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

4 August 2017 · No. 19

Railway Workers' Day: awards to the best workers and veterans

On Railway Workers' Day, which falls this year on Sunday 6 August, the management of the railway thanked its staff and veterans. Forty workers of the depot, the track distances and the stations received certificates of honour and bonuses, and ten veterans with more than forty years on the railway were given the badge of Honoured Railway Worker of the enterprise.

On Saturday 5 August the Nurzhan depot will be open to families from 10:00 to 14:00.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

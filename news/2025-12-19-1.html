Through traffic with Kazakhstan in 2026 agreed

At the annual meeting with Kazakhstan Temir Zholy the through tariffs and the numbers of wagons for 2026 were agreed for the exchange at the Talas North Terminal and the Nurzhan rail border station. The plan follows the applications of shippers received by 20 November.

Shippers will be told their monthly allocations by the commercial office before 31 December.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

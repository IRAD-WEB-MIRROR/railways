The railway under the quarantine regime

In accordance with the quarantine regime, the enterprise has taken measures to protect passengers and staff. Station halls and carriages are disinfected after every journey, staff who meet the public wear masks, and passengers are asked to wear masks and keep their distance at the ticket windows.

Freight trains and the exchange of trains with Kazakhstan at the border stations continue. Any change to passenger trains will be announced at the stations and on this site.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

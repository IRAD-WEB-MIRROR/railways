Grain season: wagons for the harvest at Kara-Bulak

From 25 August the railway is placing covered hopper wagons for grain at Kara-Bulak, beside the silos, every day. Shippers who have not yet given their September orders should do so at the commercial office by 30 August.

The Kara-Bulak goods office will work without a lunch break until the end of October.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

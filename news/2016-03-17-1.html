Nooruz: extra carriages on trains No. 1/2

For the Nooruz holiday, on 19, 20 and 22 March trains No. 1 and No. 2 will run with two extra platskart carriages. Tickets for the extra seats are on sale at the windows at Talas and Nurzhan now.

Passengers are asked to buy tickets in advance: on holiday days the trains are usually full by the evening before.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

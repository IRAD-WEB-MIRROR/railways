28 October 2021 · No. 26

The railway is ready for winter

The commission of the enterprise has accepted the railway's readiness for work in winter. Snow ploughs and snow-clearing trains have been checked at the three track distances, the points at the main stations have been fitted with heaters and the carriages of trains No. 1/2 have been prepared for heating.

In heavy snow and fog trains may run late; expected times are shown on the home page of this site.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

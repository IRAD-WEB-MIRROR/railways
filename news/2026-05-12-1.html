12 May 2026 · No. 11

New passenger timetable from 1 June

From 1 June 2026 a new passenger timetable comes into force. Train No. 1 will leave Talas at 07:40 and train No. 2 will leave Nurzhan at 08:05; the two trains cross at passing loop No. 17. The journey by the lowland line takes about seven hours.

Tickets for journeys from 1 June are on sale ten days ahead, as usual. The full timetable is on the timetable page.

Press service of Irad Temir Jolu

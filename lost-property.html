Lost property

Things left on trains are kept for a month at the station where the train ends. The list is updated every Monday.

If you see something of yours, note its number and come to the duty officer's room at the station. Describe the item; bring your passport. Things not claimed after a month go to the store of the Nurzhan Freight Depot; food is destroyed on the day. Documents are handed to the Gendarmerie and are not listed here.

Welcome to the official site of Irad Temir Jolu!

Irad Temir Jolu runs the railway of the Irad Special Economic Zone: about 690 km of line from Talas and the Sary-Bulak works to Nurzhan and the two rail crossings into Kazakhstan, the daily Talas–Nurzhan passenger train and the suburban trains of Chu District.

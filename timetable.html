Talas – Nurzhan, train No. 1/2

Daily. The train runs down the Talas valley to Aulie-Ata Junction, where the locomotive runs round, and east along the Chu line to Nurzhan. The two trains cross at passing loop No. 17. Where two times are shown, the first is arrival and the second departure.

Suburban trains in Chu District

Suburban diesel trains run west from Nurzhan along the Chu line to the villages of the district. All stop at every station.

Documents and legal acts

Orders, rules and reports of the enterprise that concern passengers, shippers and the public, given for information. Where the text on this site differs from the signed copy kept in the chancellery, the signed copy applies.

Order · 14 October 2026 · No. 112-P

On the passenger timetable from 1 November 2026

In force

Approves the passenger timetable from 1 November 2026 and the winter times of suburban trains 6005 and 6006 (1 November to 31 March).

Full text ›   Download (PDF) ›

Order · 21 September 2026 · No. 98

On preparation for work in winter 2026/27

In force

Sets the tasks and dates for the track distances, the depot and the stations: snow-clearing trains on standby from 1 November, point heaters tested by 25 October, heating of carriages from 15 October, and a commission to accept readiness by 30 October.

Report · 18 April 2024

Annual Report 2023

Published

Traffic figures for 2023: about 5.5 million tonnes of freight and about 180,000 passengers on about 690 km of line; about 5,200 staff; about 30 main-line locomotives on the books.

Order · 1 March 2023 · No. 31

Regulation on the consideration of appeals of citizens

In force

How letters, e-mails and entries in the books of complaints are registered, who answers them and in what time.

Full text ›

Order · 10 June 2022 · No. 58-G

Procedure for ordering wagons and for work on the private sidings at Nurzhan

In force

How shippers order wagons for the coming month, how wagons are placed on and taken from the private sidings of the Nurzhan Rail Freight Depot, and the free time for loading.

Full text ›

Order · 15 February 2021 · No. 19-P

Regulation on things found on trains and at stations

In force

How things left on trains and at stations are recorded, kept for a month at the station where the train ends, returned to their owners and published in a weekly list.

Full text ›

Order · 1 April 2019 · No. 47-P

Rules for the carriage of passengers, luggage and hand luggage

In force

Approves the rules for passengers on train No. 1/2 and the suburban trains: tickets, children's and pensioners' fares, hand luggage, bedding and what may not be carried.

Full text ›

Instruction · 2 July 2018 · No. 64

Instruction on safety on railway land and at level crossings

In force

Rules for the public on railway land: walking on the track, crossing it only at crossings and footpaths, driving stock over the line, stopping at crossings, and the ban on climbing onto wagons and on photography at the border stations without permission.

Charter · 21 January 2008 · No. 08/1

Charter of the State Enterprise Irad Temir Jolu

In force

Sets up the enterprise on the property of the Territorial Railway Administration, defines its purpose (the carriage of passengers and freight and the upkeep of the railway of the Zone), its management by a Director-General and its answerability to the Ministry of Transport. Approved by the Ministry of Transport.
